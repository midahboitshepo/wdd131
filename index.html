Botswana

Botswana is a landlocked country located in Southern Africa known for its diverse wildlife and stunning natural landscapes. It has a stable democracy and is one of the continent's most prosperous nations, largely due to diamond mining and tourism.The country's name means "Land of the Tswana", referring to the dominant ethnic group in Botswana. The Constitution of Botswana recognises a homogeneous Tswana state. In Setswana the native language, Batswana is grammatically plural; its singular form, which can refer to a single member of the Tswana or to a single citizen of Botswana, is Motswana.
